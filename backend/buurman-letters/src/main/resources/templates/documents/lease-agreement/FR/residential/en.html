The security deposit may not exceed 1 month’s rent excluding charges (loyer en principal); it bears no interest for the tenant and may not be revised during the performance of the agreement, even if renewed. No security deposit may be provided for where the rent is payable in advance for a period of more than 2 months; however, if the tenant requests monthly payment of the rent, the landlord may require a security deposit (article 22 of the Law of 6 July 1989).

The security deposit is returned within a maximum period of 2 months from the return of the keys to the landlord or the landlord’s agent, by hand or by registered letter with acknowledgement of receipt, after deduction, where applicable, of the sums still owed to the landlord and of the sums for which the landlord may be liable in place of the tenant, provided that they are duly justified. That period is 1 month where the check-out inventory is consistent with the check-in inventory. In a multi-dwelling building, the landlord may retain, where duly justified, a provision not exceeding 20 % of the amount of the deposit until the annual closing of the building’s accounts; the balance is returned within the month following the final approval of those accounts.

If the deposit is not returned within the periods laid down, the security deposit still owed is increased by a sum equal to 10 % of the monthly rent excluding charges for each monthly period of delay begun, unless the failure to return it results from the tenant not providing the address of his new home. If the dwelling changes hands, the obligation to return the deposit passes to the new landlord. The check-out inventory is drawn up as provided in this agreement (see article ).

During the notice period, the tenant owes rent and charges only for the time he actually occupied the dwelling if the notice was given by the landlord; if it was given by the tenant, he owes rent and charges for the whole notice period, unless the dwelling is occupied before the end of the notice period by another tenant with the landlord’s agreement.
