The tenancy is concluded for a fixed term and ends at the end of end date. The parties agree in writing that the lease expires at the end of the stipulated period without any notice of termination being required.

Under full and partial application of the MRG, the expiry is enforceable only if the originally agreed term or the extension is in each case at least 5 years or, if the landlord was not a trader within the meaning of the KSchG at the time the fixed term was agreed, at least 3 years (§ 29 para. 1 no. 3 MRG); otherwise the agreement is deemed concluded for an indefinite period (§ 29 para. 3 lit. a MRG). The agreement may be renewed in writing any number of times for at least 5 years each or, if the landlord is not a trader within the meaning of the KSchG, for at least 3 years each (§ 29 para. 4 MRG). If, after the validly agreed or extended term has expired, it is neither extended by contract nor terminated, it is deemed renewed once for 5 years or, if the landlord is not a trader within the meaning of the KSchG, for 3 years; if it is again not terminated after that, it is deemed renewed for an indefinite period (§ 29 para. 3 lit. b MRG).

Under full exemption from the MRG these restrictions do not apply; the agreement expires when the agreed period ends (§ 1113 ABGB). If the tenant continues to use the premises after expiry and the landlord lets this stand, the agreement is tacitly renewed (§ 1114 ABGB, § 1115 ABGB).

The tenant’s right to give notice during the fixed term is governed by what this agreement provides (see section ).

The main rent (Hauptmietzins) is amount monthly. In addition, the tenant pays the share of the operating costs and current public charges (see section ). If the landlord is liable to VAT, the statutory VAT is added.

Under full application of the MRG, the agreed main rent may not exceed the amount permitted under § 16 MRG. If the conditions of § 16 para. 1 MRG for a reasonable main rent are not met, the main rent is capped, for a dwelling of fixture category (Ausstattungskategorie) A, B or C, at the reasonable amount calculated on the basis of the reference value (Richtwert) (§ 16 para. 2 MRG) and, for a dwelling of fixture category D, at the amounts under § 16 para. 5 MRG. A location surcharge (Lagezuschlag) is permitted only if the property is in a better than average location and the circumstances relevant to the location surcharge were expressly disclosed to the tenant in writing no later than when the lease was concluded (§ 16 para. 4 MRG).

Under full application of the MRG, rent agreements are invalid to the extent that the agreed main rent exceeds the permitted maximum; the invalidity must be asserted within 3 years before the court or the municipality (Gemeinde), and for fixed-term main tenancies this period ends no earlier than 6 months after the tenancy ends or is converted into a tenancy for an indefinite period, and the limitation period is then 10 years (§ 16 para. 8 MRG). For a fixed-term main tenancy agreement, the maximum main rent permitted under § 16 para. 1 to 6 MRG is reduced by 25 percent (§ 16 para. 7 MRG).

Under partial application and under full exemption from the MRG, the amount of the main rent is freely agreed. Under full application, the agreements listed in § 27 para. 1 MRG, in particular on payments without equivalent consideration (Ablösen), are void and prohibited.

The indexation of the main rent is governed by what this agreement provides (see section ).

The main rent is index-linked in accordance with § 1 para. 2 of the Rents Value Protection Act (Mieten-Wertsicherungsgesetz, MieWeG). It accordingly increases or decreases on 1 April of the full calendar year after the agreement is concluded and thereafter every year on 1 April by the average change of the Consumer Price Index 2020 (Verbraucherpreisindex), or of the index replacing it, in the preceding calendar year; if that change exceeds 3 percent, the part exceeding 3 percentage points is taken into account only by half. At the first indexation, the change is taken into account only in the ratio of the full months of the preceding calendar year that elapsed after the agreement was concluded to 12 months (§ 1 para. 2 no. 1 and 2 MieWeG).

If the tenancy is subject to the rent restrictions of the MRG, the average change of the Consumer Price Index is taken into account at most at 1 percent for the year 2025 and at most at 2 percent for the year 2026 (§ 1 para. 3 MieWeG). If the indexation results in a main rent higher than permitted under § 16 para. 1 to 7 MRG, the excess is invalid; the increased main rent is payable from the rent due date (Zinstermin) following the date on which the index change takes effect, if the landlord announces the request for the increase (Erhöhungsbegehren) in a letter sent after the index change takes effect, but no later than 14 days before that due date (§ 16 para. 9 MRG).

Under full exemption from the MRG, the MieWeG does not apply by law; the parties agree the above indexation for that case as well (§ 2 MieWeG). The indexation operates both upwards and downwards.

The tenant bears the share of the operating costs (Betriebskosten) and the current public charges of the property attributable to the leased premises and pays monthly instalments towards them together with the main rent, in the amount stated in this agreement.

Under full application of the MRG, only the costs listed in §§ 21 to 24 MRG may be charged; the share is in principle determined by the ratio of the usable floor area of the leased premises to the usable floor area of all leased units of the building (§ 17 para. 1 MRG). The landlord may charge on each rent due date a constant instalment calculated from the total amount of the preceding calendar year, which may be exceeded by at most 10 percent in the event of an interim increase; the landlord settles the account no later than 30 June of the following calendar year and grants inspection of the supporting documents. A surplus must be refunded on the next rent due date but one, and a shortfall must be paid on the next rent due date but one (§ 21 para. 3 MRG).

To secure the landlord’s future claims under this lease, the tenant hands over to the landlord a deposit (Kaution) in the amount agreed in writing between the parties.

There is no statutory maximum for the amount of the deposit. If the deposit is handed over as a sum of money, the landlord invests it at interest in a savings book (Sparbuch) and informs the tenant of this in writing on request; another form of investment is permitted if it offers equally good interest and the same level of security as a savings deposit and allows a clear separation from the landlord’s assets and, in the landlord’s insolvency, separate satisfaction (§ 16b para. 1 MRG).

After the lease ends, the landlord returns the deposit to the tenant without undue delay, together with the interest earned on its investment, insofar as it is not used to settle justified claims of the landlord arising from the tenancy (§ 16b para. 2 MRG). § 16b MRG applies under full and partial application of the MRG; under full exemption the parties agree the same rules. Damage is established when the leased premises are returned (see section ).

The tenant pays the rent (see section ) together with the instalments on the operating costs monthly in advance, no later than day 5 of the payment period concernedno later than day 5 of the payment period concerned, by transfer to a bank account notified by the landlord.

Under full application of the MRG, the rent is payable in advance on the 5th of each calendar month unless a later payment date has been agreed (§ 15 para. 3 MRG). For the lease of premises, the rent is payable monthly on the 5th of the month unless otherwise agreed or customary in the locality (§ 1100 ABGB).

In the event of late payment, the tenant owes default interest of 4 percent per year (§ 1000 para. 1 ABGB, § 1333 para. 1 ABGB) and compensation for the necessary costs of appropriate out-of-court collection or recovery measures, insofar as the tenant is at fault for them and they are reasonably proportionate to the claim pursued (§ 1333 para. 2 ABGB).

The leased premises may be used for residential purposes only. The tenant treats them with care and shows consideration for the other occupants of the building; any house rules (Hausordnung) form part of this agreement insofar as they do not conflict with it.

The tenant allows the landlord, or persons instructed by the landlord, to enter the leased premises for important reasons after timely notice, with the tenant’s legitimate interests being duly taken into account according to the importance of the reason; under full application of the MRG, § 8 para. 2 MRG applies to this.

The tenant makes material alterations to the leased premises only with the landlord’s prior written consent. Under full application of the MRG, the tenant notifies the landlord of an intended material alteration; if the landlord does not refuse it within 2 months after receiving the notification, consent is deemed given, and under the conditions of § 9 para. 1 MRG the landlord cannot refuse it. Compensation for expenditure when the tenancy ends is governed there by § 10 MRG.

Subletting all or part of the leased premises, or otherwise letting third parties use them, requires the landlord’s prior written consent.

Under full application of the MRG, the landlord may rely on this prohibition only if there is an important reason against the subletting, in particular if the leased premises are to be sublet in their entirety, the proposed sub-rent is a disproportionately high consideration compared with the rent payable by the tenant and any other services of the tenant, the number of occupants exceeds the number of living rooms or would exceed it after the subtenant moves in, or there is good reason to fear that the subtenant will disturb the peace of the household community of the building (§ 11 para. 1 MRG).

Under partial application and under full exemption from the MRG, the contractual prohibition of subletting applies (§ 1098 ABGB). Under full and partial application of the MRG, passing on the leased premises may, under the conditions of § 30 para. 2 no. 4 MRG, constitute a ground for termination.

The landlord hands over the leased premises at the landlord’s own expense in a usable condition and maintains them in that condition (§ 1096 para. 1 ABGB). Under full application of the MRG, the landlord’s maintenance duty is governed by § 3 MRG; it covers in particular the maintenance of the common parts of the building, the remedying of serious damage to the building, the removal of a considerable health hazard emanating from the leased premises and the maintenance of gas boilers, hot-water boilers and other heating appliances let with the premises (§ 3 para. 2 MRG).

If the leased premises are so defective on handover, or become so defective during the lease term without the tenant’s fault, that they are unfit for the stipulated use, the tenant is released from paying the rent for the duration and to the extent of the unfitness; this release cannot be waived in advance (§ 1096 para. 1 ABGB).

If repairs become necessary that are the landlord’s responsibility, the tenant notifies the landlord without delay (§ 1097 ABGB). Under full application of the MRG, the tenant services the leased premises and the installations intended for them, in particular the electricity, gas, water, heating and sanitary installations, and keeps them in repair, except where serious damage to the building is to be remedied or a considerable health hazard removed, so that no disadvantage arises for the landlord and the other tenants of the building (§ 8 para. 1 MRG).

The tenant must allow necessary repairs (§ 1118 ABGB); under full application of the MRG, the toleration of interference with the tenancy right is governed by § 8 para. 2 and 3 MRG. The tenant is liable for damage and for wear caused by misuse through the tenant’s own fault and through the fault of a subtenant, but not for chance events (§ 1111 ABGB).

The landlord presented to the tenant, in good time before the tenant made the contractual declaration, an energy performance certificate (Energieausweis) no more than 10 years old at that time and hands it over to the tenant within 14 days after the agreement is concluded, on paper if the tenant so requests (§ 4 para. 1 EAVG 2012, Energy Performance Certificate Presentation Act, Energieausweis-Vorlage-Gesetz). The energy indicators stated in it are deemed a stipulated characteristic, taking into account the margins that are unavoidable in determining them (§ 6 EAVG 2012).

If no energy performance certificate was presented, at least an overall energy efficiency corresponding to the age and type of the building is deemed agreed (§ 7 para. 1 EAVG 2012). If the certificate is not handed over after the agreement is concluded despite a request, the tenant may enforce the handover in court or obtain a certificate and claim the reasonable costs from the landlord within 3 years after the agreement is concluded (§ 7 para. 2 EAVG 2012).

The tenant registers with the registration authority (Meldebehörde) within 3 days after taking up accommodation in the leased premises (§ 3 para. 1 MeldeG). The landlord, as accommodation provider (Unterkunftgeber), cooperates in the registration insofar as the Registration Act 1991 (Meldegesetz) so provides.

When the leased premises are handed over, the parties jointly draw up a handover report (Übergabeprotokoll) on the condition of the leased premises, the meter readings and the keys handed over. Both parties sign the report and each receives a copy.

After the tenancy ends, the tenant returns the leased premises with all keys in the condition in which the tenant received them (§ 1109 ABGB); the parties then draw up a return report. The landlord’s claims for damage or wear caused by misuse must be asserted in court no later than within 1 year after the return (§ 1111 ABGB); the tenant’s claims for expenditure on the leased premises no later than within 6 months after the return (§ 1097 ABGB). The return of the deposit follows (see section ).

Under full and partial application of the MRG, the tenant may terminate the lease by notice given through the court or in writing, the landlord, however, only through the court (§ 33 para. 1 MRG). The landlord may give notice only for important reasons, in particular those listed in § 30 para. 2 MRG, and states the grounds for termination briefly in the notice (§ 30 para. 1 MRG, § 33 para. 1 MRG). An agreement under which the landlord is to have the right to give notice without restriction or to a greater extent than the law provides is void (§ 30 para. 3 MRG).

Unless the parties agree otherwise, notice of termination (Aufkündigung) of a lease for an indefinite period must be given for the last day of a calendar month in such a way that the notice is served on the other party, where rent is paid at monthly or shorter intervals, no later than 1 month and, where rent is paid at longer intervals, no later than 3 months before the termination date (§ 560 para. 1 no. 2 lit. d ZPO). If a written notice from the tenant reaches the landlord only after the notice period has begun, it takes effect for the first later termination date for which the period was still open (§ 33 para. 1 MRG).

For a lease with a fixed term under § 29 para. 1 no. 3 MRG (see section ), the tenant has, after 1 year of the originally agreed or extended term of the tenancy, the inalienable and unrestrictable right to terminate the lease before the stipulated period expires, in each case for the last day of a month, through the court or in writing, subject to a notice period of 3 months (§ 29 para. 2 MRG); the tenant may terminate an agreement renewed under § 29 para. 3 lit. b MRG at any time in the same way.

Under full exemption from the MRG, the restrictions on termination in §§ 29 to 36 MRG do not apply; either party may terminate a lease for an indefinite period in writing or through the court, observing the agreed termination dates and periods or those laid down in § 560 ZPO.

The tenant may withdraw from the agreement without notice if the leased premises are handed over in a condition, or come without the tenant’s fault into a condition, that makes them unfit for the stipulated use, or if a considerable part is withdrawn or becomes unusable by chance for a longer period; on the ground that rented living rooms are harmful to health, the tenant has this right even if the tenant waived it or knew the condition of the rooms when the agreement was concluded (§ 1117 ABGB). The landlord may demand early cancellation of the agreement in particular if the tenant makes considerably detrimental use of the leased premises or, after a payment reminder, is in default with paying the rent to such an extent that the tenant has not paid the arrears in full when the due date expires (§ 1118 ABGB).
