A lease of indefinite term ends by notice of termination given in writing. Where the lease falls within Presidential Decree 34/1995, the notice takes legal effect 3 months after it is served, but not before the minimum term of 3 years has elapsed (article 13, paragraph 1, of Law 4242/2014); otherwise, where the rent is set by the month, notice is given, unless otherwise agreed, at least 15 days in advance for the end of the calendar month (article 609 of the Civil Code).

If the tenant is in arrears with the rent, wholly or in part, the landlord is entitled to terminate the lease by notice given at least 1 month in advance in the case of a lease whose term was agreed for 1 year or more, and 10 days in advance in other leases, without prejudice to the landlord’s claim for damages for the early ending of the lease; the notice has no effect if the tenant, before that period expires, pays the rent in arrears together with any costs of the notice (article 597 of the Civil Code). Any agreement that shortens these periods, or ends the lease automatically, or gives the landlord such a right as soon as the tenant is in default with the payment of the rent, is void (article 598 of the Civil Code).

The tenant is entitled to terminate the lease if the agreed use was not granted to the tenant unhindered in good time, wholly or in part, or was later withdrawn, after setting the landlord a reasonable period to restore it which has expired without result, or even without setting a period if, because of that ground, the tenant has no interest in the performance of the agreement, save in the cases for which the landlord is not liable (articles 585 and 586 of the Civil Code). On the tenant’s death, the tenant’s heirs are entitled to terminate the lease by notice given at least 3 months in advance, taking effect at the end of a calendar month (article 612 of the Civil Code).

The landlord may apply for an order for possession (διαταγή απόδοσης της χρήσης μισθίου) in the event of arrears of rent due to obstinacy (δυστροπία) or of the expiry of the fixed term of the lease, under the conditions of article 637 of the Code of Civil Procedure (Κώδικας Πολιτικής Δικονομίας, ΚΠολΔ): the start or the end of the lease must be proved in writing and a written demand must have been served by a judicial officer at least 15 days before the application is filed, which is required only the first time; payment of the rent within that period, proved in writing, prevents the order from being issued, unless there are repeated arrears due to obstinacy.
