This agreement is governed by Chapter 12 of the Land Code (jordabalken, JB; the Tenancy Act, hyreslagen). A dwelling (bostadslägenhet) means premises let to be used, wholly or to a not insignificant extent, as a home (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 1 b, paragraph 1). Contract terms that conflict with a provision of Chapter 12 of the Land Code have no effect against the tenant or against a person entitled to take the tenant’s place, unless otherwise provided (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 1 d). The lease must be drawn up in writing if the landlord or the tenant so requests (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 2, paragraph 1).

This agreement is intended for a first-hand letting of a whole dwelling that is not part of the landlord’s own home. It is not intended for leases governed by the Private Letting Act (privatuthyrningslagen, 2026:772) instead of Chapter 12 of the Land Code (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 1 c). That Act applies where a natural person or an estate lets a home, unless the landlord regularly lets more than 2 dwellings that are not part of the landlord’s own home, holds the dwelling under a tenancy (hyresrätt) or the letting is for holiday purposes (Private Letting Act, Chapter 1, section 1 and section 3).

The tenancy begins on start date.

The lease runs until further notice (for an indefinite term) and must be terminated by notice in order to end (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 3, paragraph 1).

The lease is concluded for a fixed term up to and including end date. A fixed-term lease ends when the term expires, unless otherwise agreed. Where the tenancy has lasted more than 9 months in a row, however, the lease must always be terminated by notice in order to end (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 3, paragraph 2).

The lease is also deemed extended for an indefinite term if the tenant, although the lease has ended without notice, has continued to use the dwelling 1 month after the end of the term without the landlord having requested the tenant to move out (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 3, paragraph 3, point 2).

The rent is amount (per month). The rent for a dwelling must be stated as a fixed amount in the lease (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 19, paragraph 1). Charges for heating, hot water, electricity and water and sewerage are governed separately by this agreement (see Article ).

If the landlord and the tenant disagree on the amount of the rent, the rent is to be set at a reasonable amount. The rent is not to be regarded as reasonable if it is appreciably higher than the rent for dwellings that are equivalent in terms of their utility value (bruksvärde) (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 55, paragraph 1). In that assessment, rents for dwellings set in negotiated agreements under the Tenancy Negotiation Act (hyresförhandlingslagen, 1978:304) are to be taken into account primarily (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 55, paragraph 2).

This agreement contains no negotiation clause (förhandlingsklausul) under the Tenancy Negotiation Act and is not intended for a rent set in a negotiated agreement under section 19 a or section 19 b of that Act (presumptive rent, presumtionshyra).

Any person who demands, agrees to or accepts a special payment for letting a dwelling or for transferring the tenancy of such a dwelling commits a criminal offence, and a contract term providing for a special payment is void (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 65).

Changes to the rent are governed by this agreement (see Article ).

The rent may be changed by agreement between the parties. If the landlord or the tenant wishes the terms of the lease to be changed, that party must notify the other party in writing. If no agreement can be reached, the party is entitled to apply to the Regional Rent Tribunal (hyresnämnden) for a change of the terms; the application may be made at the earliest 1 month after the notice was given to the other party (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 54, paragraph 1).

A decision of the Regional Rent Tribunal changing the terms of the lease counts as an agreement on the terms of the continued letting (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 54, paragraph 3). Such a decision may not cover the period before the points in time set out in Chapter 12, section 55 e of the Land Code.

Since the rent must be stated as a fixed amount in the lease (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 19, paragraph 1), this agreement contains no term under which the rent changes automatically in line with an index.

The dwelling is let to be used as a home for the tenant and the tenant’s household. The tenant may not use the dwelling for any purpose other than the intended one; the landlord may not, however, rely on deviations that are of no significance to the landlord (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 23, paragraph 1).

The tenant must ensure that what is required to preserve safety, health, order and good condition within the property is not disregarded and that those living in the surroundings are not exposed to disturbances that may be so harmful to health or otherwise so detrimental to their living environment that they should not reasonably have to tolerate them (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 25). The tenant may not, without the landlord’s consent, sublet the dwelling or otherwise make it available second-hand to another person for independent use (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 39, paragraph 1). The tenant may sublet the dwelling if the Regional Rent Tribunal grants permission. Permission must be granted if the tenant has good reasons for the subletting owing to special family circumstances, age, illness, temporary work or studies elsewhere, a longer stay abroad or comparable circumstances, if the landlord has no justified reason to refuse consent and if the rent does not exceed a reasonable rent under Chapter 12, section 55, paragraph 4, of the Land Code. Permission must be limited to a certain period and may be subject to conditions (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 40).

The tenant may not transfer the tenancy without the landlord’s consent (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 32, paragraph 1). If consent is refused without reasonable cause, or if the landlord gives no answer within 3 weeks after consent was requested, the tenant may terminate the lease (Land Code, Chapter 12, section 32, paragraph 2).
