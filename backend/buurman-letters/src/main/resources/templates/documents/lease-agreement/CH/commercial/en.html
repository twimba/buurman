An agreement that the rent follows an index is valid only if the lease is concluded for at least 5 years and the index used is the Swiss consumer price index (Article 269b CO); the lease is concluded for 5 years if the Landlord cannot terminate it for at least 5 years (Article 17 paragraph 4 VMWG). Subject to a challenge of the initial rent, a party may argue before the conciliation authority only that the increase or reduction requested is not justified by a corresponding change in the index (Article 270c CO).

An agreement that the rent increases periodically by a fixed amount (graduated rent) is valid only if the lease is concluded for at least 3 years, the rent is increased no more than 1 time per year and the amount of the increase is fixed in Swiss francs (Article 269c CO). Written form suffices for notifying such an increase (Article 269d paragraph 5 CO); the notification may be made at the earliest 4 months before the increase takes effect (Article 19a VMWG). Subject to a challenge of the initial rent, the Tenant cannot challenge graduated rents (Article 270d CO).

Otherwise, the Landlord may increase the rent with effect from the next possible termination date; the Landlord must notify the Tenant of the rent increase, with reasons, at least 10 days before the notice period begins, on a form approved by the canton (Article 269d paragraph 1 CO). The rent increase is void if the Landlord does not notify it on the prescribed form, does not give reasons for it, or threatens or gives notice of termination together with the notification (Article 269d paragraph 2 CO). The Tenant may challenge a rent increase as abusive before the conciliation authority within 30 days after being notified of it (Article 270b paragraph 1 CO) and request a reduction of the rent under the conditions of Article 270a CO.
