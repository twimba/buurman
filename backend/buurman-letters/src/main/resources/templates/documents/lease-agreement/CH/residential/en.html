the following lease of residential premises is concluded. Where several persons are tenants, they are jointly and severally liable for all obligations under this agreement.

The dwelling serves the Tenant and the Tenant's spouse or registered partner as the family home (Familienwohnung): yes / no (please indicate as applicable). Name of the spouse or registered partner: ______________________________

The Landlord lets to the Tenant, for residential purposes, the dwelling at Address, consisting of ________ rooms, floor ________, together with the following ancillary rooms and facilities (cellar, attic, parking spaces, furniture): ______________________________, hereinafter “the Premises”. The provisions on the lease of residential premises also apply to the items that the Landlord lets for use together with the dwelling (Article 253a paragraph 1 CO).

This agreement is governed by the provisions of the Swiss Code of Obligations on leases (Article 253 et seq. CO, Obligationenrecht) and by the Ordinance on the Lease of Residential and Commercial Premises (VMWG). Where those provisions render a deviating agreement void, they prevail over the deviating provisions of this agreement.

The provisions on protection against abusive rents do not apply to the lease of luxury flats and single-family houses with 6 or more living rooms, not counting the kitchen (Article 253b paragraph 2 CO, Article 2 paragraph 1 VMWG). The provisions on the lease of residential premises do not apply to holiday flats let for 3 months or less (Article 253a paragraph 2 CO).

The lease is concluded for an indefinite period and may be terminated by either party in accordance with this agreement and the law (Article 255 paragraph 1 CO, Article 266a paragraph 1 CO).

The lease is concluded for a fixed term and ends without notice on End date. A fixed-term lease ends without notice when the agreed term expires (Article 255 paragraph 2 CO, Article 266 paragraph 1 CO).

If the parties tacitly continue the lease after the agreed term has expired, it is deemed a lease for an indefinite period (Article 266 paragraph 2 CO).

The Tenant may challenge the initial rent as abusive before the conciliation authority (Schlichtungsbehörde) within 30 days after taking over the Premises and request its reduction if the Tenant felt compelled to conclude the lease because of personal or family hardship or because of the conditions on the local market for residential and commercial premises, or if the Landlord has considerably increased the initial rent compared with the previous rent for the same premises (Article 270 paragraph 1 CO). The Tenant may request to be informed of the amount of the rent under the previous lease (Article 256a paragraph 2 CO).

In the canton Canton, according to the list of the Federal Office for Housing (BWO) for the year 2026, the obligation to use the form referred to in Article 270 paragraph 2 CO when concluding a new lease of residential premises applies throughout the canton (Formularpflicht). The Landlord therefore notifies the Tenant of the initial rent on the form approved by the canton. That form states in particular the reference interest rate and the Swiss consumer price index that applied to the previous rent (Article 19 paragraph 3 VMWG). The obligation depends on the vacancy rate and may change; the cantonal law in force when the lease is concluded is decisive.

In the canton Canton, according to the list of the Federal Office for Housing (BWO) for the year 2026, the obligation to use the form referred to in Article 270 paragraph 2 CO when concluding a new lease of residential premises (Formularpflicht) applies only in part of the canton, namely in certain districts or municipalities and, where applicable, only to certain dwelling sizes. If the dwelling is located in such an area, the Landlord notifies the Tenant of the initial rent on the form approved by the canton. That form states in particular the reference interest rate and the Swiss consumer price index that applied to the previous rent (Article 19 paragraph 3 VMWG). The obligation depends on the vacancy rate and may change; the cantonal law in force when the lease is concluded is decisive.

In the event of a housing shortage, the cantons may make the use of the official form compulsory for the conclusion of any new lease in all or part of their territory (Article 270 paragraph 2 CO). If such an obligation (Formularpflicht) applies where the dwelling is located, the Landlord notifies the Tenant of the initial rent on the form approved by the canton. That form states in particular the reference interest rate and the Swiss consumer price index that applied to the previous rent (Article 19 paragraph 3 VMWG). The cantonal law in force when the lease is concluded is decisive.

Changes to the rent are governed by the provisions of this agreement (see article ).

The Landlord may increase the rent at any time with effect from the next possible termination date. The Landlord must notify the Tenant of the rent increase, with reasons, at least 10 days before the notice period begins, on a form approved by the canton (Article 269d paragraph 1 CO). The rent increase is void if the Landlord does not notify it on the prescribed form, does not give reasons for it, or threatens or gives notice of termination together with the notification (Article 269d paragraph 2 CO). The same applies if the Landlord intends to amend the agreement unilaterally in any other way to the detriment of the Tenant, in particular by reducing the Landlord's existing services or introducing new ancillary costs (Article 269d paragraph 3 CO).

Rents are abusive if they yield an excessive return from the leased property or are based on a manifestly excessive purchase price (Article 269 CO); the exceptions are governed by Article 269a CO and, for changes in the mortgage reference interest rate, by Article 12a VMWG and Article 13 VMWG. The Tenant may challenge a rent increase as abusive before the conciliation authority within 30 days after being notified of it (Article 270b paragraph 1 CO).

If the Tenant has reason to believe that, because of a substantial change in the basis of calculation, in particular a reduction in costs, the Landlord obtains an excessive return from the leased property, the Tenant may request a reduction of the rent with effect from the next possible termination date (Article 270a paragraph 1 CO). The Tenant submits the request for reduction in writing to the Landlord, who must respond within 30 days; if the Landlord does not grant the request, grants it only in part or does not respond in time, the Tenant may refer the matter to the conciliation authority within 30 days (Article 270a paragraph 2 CO).

Agreed indexation or graduated increases of the rent: ______________________________ (none if nothing is stated).

An agreement that the rent follows an index is valid only if the lease is concluded for at least 5 years and the index used is the Swiss consumer price index (Article 269b CO); the lease is concluded for 5 years if the Landlord cannot terminate it for at least 5 years (Article 17 paragraph 4 VMWG). For a dwelling, each increase may not exceed the rise in the consumer price index, and if the index falls the rent must be adjusted accordingly (Article 17 paragraphs 1 and 2 VMWG). The increase may be announced with at least 30 days' notice to the end of a month (Article 17 paragraph 3 VMWG); it is notified on the official form, at the earliest after the new index figure has been published (Article 19 paragraph 2 VMWG).

An agreement that the rent increases periodically by a fixed amount (graduated rent) is valid only if the lease is concluded for at least 3 years, the rent is increased no more than 1 time per year and the amount of the increase is fixed in Swiss francs (Article 269c CO). Written form suffices for notifying such an increase (Article 269d paragraph 5 CO); the notification may be made at the earliest 4 months before the increase takes effect (Article 19a VMWG). Subject to a challenge of the initial rent, the Tenant cannot challenge graduated rents (Article 270d CO).

Ancillary costs are the Landlord's actual expenses for services connected with the use of the Premises, such as heating, hot water and similar operating costs, and for public charges arising from the use of the Premises (Article 257b paragraph 1 CO). The Tenant makes advance payments on account of ________ Swiss francs per month; if the Landlord charges the ancillary costs on the basis of a statement, the Landlord prepares it at least 1 time per year and presents it to the Tenant (Article 4 paragraph 1 VMWG). If the Landlord charges them as a flat rate, the Landlord bases it on average figures over 3 years (Article 4 paragraph 2 VMWG).

On request, the Landlord allows the Tenant to inspect the supporting documents (Article 257b paragraph 2 CO).

The Tenant provides the Landlord with security for the Tenant's obligations (rent deposit, Mietkaution) of Amount.

The Tenant provides the Landlord with security for the Tenant's obligations (rent deposit, Mietkaution) in the amount agreed in writing between the parties.

In the lease of residential premises, the Landlord may require security of no more than 3 months' rent (Article 257e paragraph 2 CO). The parties consider that any part of the agreed security exceeding this maximum is not owed.

If the Tenant provides the security in cash or in securities, the Landlord deposits it with a bank in a savings account or a custody account in the Tenant's name (Article 257e paragraph 1 CO). The bank may release the security only with the consent of both parties or on the basis of a final order to pay (Zahlungsbefehl) or a final court judgment. If, within 1 year after the end of the lease, the Landlord has not asserted a claim against the Tenant by legal means, the Tenant may request the bank to refund the security (Article 257e paragraph 3 CO). Supplementary cantonal provisions are reserved (Article 257e paragraph 4 CO). Any defects are established when the Premises are returned (see article ).

The Tenant pays the rent (see article ) and the ancillary costs per month, no later than day 1 of each payment periodin advance for the 1st day of each payment period, into the account designated in writing by the Landlord. This arrangement takes precedence over the statutory rule that the rent is payable at the end of each month (Article 257c CO).

When the agreed payment date has passed, the Tenant is in default without a reminder (Article 102 paragraph 2 CO) and owes default interest of 5 per cent per year (Article 104 paragraph 1 CO).

If, after taking over the Premises, the Tenant is in arrears with rent or ancillary costs that have fallen due, the Landlord may set the Tenant a payment deadline of at least 30 days in writing and warn that the lease will be terminated if the deadline expires unused; if the Tenant does not pay within that deadline, the Landlord may terminate the lease with at least 30 days' notice to the end of a month (Article 257d paragraphs 1 and 2 CO). Neither party may waive in advance the right to set off claims and debts arising from the lease (Article 265 CO).

The Premises may be used for residential purposes only. The Tenant must use the Premises with care and show consideration for the other occupants of the building and the neighbours (Article 257f paragraphs 1 and 2 CO). Any house rules form part of this agreement insofar as they do not contradict it.

The Tenant must allow the Landlord to inspect the Premises to the extent necessary for maintenance, sale or re-letting; the Landlord announces inspections in good time and takes the Tenant's interests into account (Article 257h paragraphs 2 and 3 CO).

The Tenant may sublet all or part of the Premises with the Landlord's consent (Article 262 paragraph 1 CO). The Tenant requests the Landlord's consent in writing and discloses the terms of the sublease at the same time.

The Landlord may refuse consent only if the Tenant refuses to disclose the terms of the sublease, if the terms of the sublease are abusive compared with those of the main lease, or if the sublease would cause the Landlord substantial disadvantages (Article 262 paragraph 2 CO).

The Tenant is liable to the Landlord for ensuring that the subtenant does not use the Premises in any way other than that permitted to the Tenant; the Landlord may require the subtenant directly to comply (Article 262 paragraph 3 CO). A sublease may be extended only for the duration of the main lease (Article 273b paragraph 1 CO).

The Landlord hands over the Premises on the agreed date in a condition fit for the intended use and maintains them in that condition (Article 256 paragraph 1 CO); agreements deviating from this to the detriment of the Tenant are void in leases of residential premises (Article 256 paragraph 2 CO). The Tenant remedies at the Tenant's own expense, in accordance with local custom, defects that can be remedied by minor cleaning or repairs required for ordinary maintenance (Article 259 CO).

The Tenant reports to the Landlord any defects that the Tenant is not required to remedy; if the Tenant fails to report them, the Tenant is liable for the resulting damage to the Landlord (Article 257g CO). If defects arise for which the Tenant is neither responsible nor required to remedy at the Tenant's own expense, or if the Tenant is disturbed in the contractual use of the Premises, the Tenant may assert the rights under Article 259a CO, in particular the remedying of the defect and a proportionate reduction of the rent from the time the Landlord became aware of the defect until it is remedied (Article 259d CO).

If the Tenant demands that a defect be remedied, the Tenant must set the Landlord a reasonable deadline in writing; the Tenant may warn the Landlord that, if the deadline expires unused, rents falling due in the future will be deposited with an office designated by the canton, and must announce the deposit to the Landlord in writing (Article 259g paragraph 1 CO). Deposited rents pass to the Landlord if the Tenant has not asserted the Tenant's claims before the conciliation authority within 30 days after the first deposited rent fell due (Article 259h paragraph 1 CO).

The Tenant tolerates work on the Premises where it is necessary to remedy defects or to repair or prevent damage; the Landlord announces such work in good time (Article 257h paragraphs 1 and 3 CO). The Landlord may carry out renovations and alterations only if they are reasonable for the Tenant and the lease has not been terminated (Article 260 paragraph 1 CO). The Tenant may carry out renovations and alterations to the Premises only with the Landlord's written consent (Article 260a paragraph 1 CO); restoration and any compensation are governed by Article 260a paragraphs 2 and 3 CO.

When the Premises are handed over, the parties jointly draw up a handover report recording the condition of the Premises, the meter readings and the keys handed over; both parties sign it and each receives a copy. The Tenant may report to the Landlord in writing, within 14 days after the handover, defects not listed in the report. If a return report was drawn up at the end of the previous lease, the Landlord shows it to the Tenant on request at the handover (Article 256a paragraph 1 CO).

At the end of the lease, the Tenant returns the Premises in the condition resulting from contractual use (Article 267 paragraph 1 CO). Agreements by which the Tenant undertakes in advance to pay, at the end of the lease, compensation covering anything other than any damage are void (Article 267 paragraph 2 CO).

On the return, the Landlord inspects the condition of the Premises and immediately notifies the Tenant of defects for which the Tenant is responsible; if the Landlord fails to do so, the Landlord loses the claims, except for defects that could not be detected by a customary inspection, and the Landlord notifies the Tenant of such defects immediately upon discovering them (Article 267a paragraphs 1 to 3 CO). The refund of the rent deposit is governed by the provisions of this agreement (see article ).

Either party may terminate the lease for an indefinite period with at least 3 months' notice to the following termination dates: ______________________________. If none are stated, notice may be given to a termination date customary in the locality or, if there is no local custom, to the end of a 3-month lease period (Article 266c CO). If a party does not observe the notice period or the termination date, the notice takes effect on the next possible termination date (Article 266a paragraph 2 CO).

The fixed-term lease (see article ) ends without notice when the agreed term expires; ordinary termination before that date is possible only if the parties have agreed it. The rights of extraordinary termination are reserved.

If the dwelling serves as the family home, a spouse may terminate the lease only with the express consent of the other spouse (Article 266m paragraph 1 CO), and the Landlord serves the notice of termination and any payment deadline with a threat of termination separately on the Tenant and on the Tenant's spouse or registered partner (Article 266n CO); these rules apply by analogy to registered partnerships (Article 266m paragraph 3 CO). A notice of termination that does not meet these formal requirements is void (Article 266o CO).

The termination may be challenged if it breaches the principle of good faith, and reasons must be given on request (Article 271 paragraphs 1 and 2 CO); termination by the Landlord may be challenged in particular in the cases set out in Article 271a CO. A party wishing to challenge the termination must file the request with the conciliation authority within 30 days after receiving the notice (Article 273 paragraph 1 CO).

The Tenant may request an extension of the lease (Erstreckung) if ending the lease would cause hardship for the Tenant or the Tenant's family that cannot be justified by the Landlord's interests (Article 272 paragraph 1 CO); extension is excluded in the cases set out in Article 272a CO. A lease of residential premises may be extended by at most 4 years; within this maximum, 1 or 2 extensions may be granted (Article 272b paragraph 1 CO). The request must be filed with the conciliation authority within 30 days after receipt of the notice for a lease for an indefinite period, and at the latest 60 days before the term expires for a fixed-term lease (Article 273 paragraph 2 CO).

Extraordinary termination is governed by the law, in particular in the event of the Tenant's payment arrears (Article 257d CO), a serious breach of the duty of care and consideration (Article 257f paragraphs 3 and 4 CO), for good cause making performance of the agreement unreasonable for a party (Article 266g CO) and, on the Tenant's death, by the Tenant's heirs (Article 266i CO). If the Landlord sells the Premises, the lease passes to the acquirer, who may terminate it with the statutory notice period to the next statutory termination date if the acquirer claims an urgent need for personal use by the acquirer, close relatives or relatives by marriage (Article 261 paragraphs 1 and 2 CO).

If the Tenant returns the Premises without observing the notice period or termination date, the Tenant is released from the Tenant's obligations towards the Landlord only by proposing a new tenant who is reasonably acceptable to the Landlord, solvent and willing to take over the lease on the same terms (Article 264 paragraph 1 CO). The Tenant may waive rights under the provisions on protection against termination only where those provisions expressly allow it (Article 273c paragraph 1 CO). The return of the Premises is governed by the provisions of this agreement (see article ).

In disputes arising from this lease, the court proceedings are preceded by an attempt at conciliation before the conciliation authority (Article 197 ZPO, Civil Procedure Code); that authority consists of a chairperson and equal representation of both sides (Article 200 paragraph 1 ZPO). In the conciliation proceedings, no court costs are charged in disputes arising from the lease of residential premises (Article 113 paragraph 2 letter c ZPO).

Actions arising from this lease fall within the jurisdiction of the court at the place where the property is located (Article 33 ZPO); the Tenant cannot waive this place of jurisdiction in advance or by entering an appearance (Article 35 paragraph 1 letter b ZPO).
