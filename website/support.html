Common questions

How do I get help?

Email hello@buurman.io. It goes to the people who build Buurman, not to a ticket queue. Tell us your team name and, if something broke, roughly when. That’s usually enough for us to find it in the logs.

How quickly will someone answer?

A working day for ordinary questions. If Buurman is down or your numbers look wrong, we treat that as urgent and come back the same day.

Is Buurman down, or is it me?

Check status.buurman.io first. It updates itself and shows any incident we’re in the middle of. If it’s green and Buurman is still misbehaving for you, write to us.

Can I get my data out?

Whenever you like, without asking us. Export any list to CSV or Excel, push the portfolio into Google Sheets, download PDF reports, or take a full Buurman Takeout of everything your team has stored.

How do I cancel or get a refund?

Cancel from Settings. There’s no notice period. Paid plans get a full refund within 30 days of payment, no questions asked, and the refund policy has the detail.

Where is my data stored?

On servers in the European Union, encrypted in transit and at rest. The privacy policy says what we collect, why, and what rights you have over it under GDPR.

I found a bug. What helps you fix it fastest?

The page you were on, what you expected, what happened instead, and a screenshot. Your browser and roughly what time it was help too.

Can I ask for a feature?

Please do. Buurman is built for people with small portfolios, and most of what ships started as one of those emails. The changelog shows what has landed so far.

Still stuck?

Write to hello@buurman.io and tell us what you were trying to do. If it’s easier to show than describe, send a screenshot. You won’t get a ticket number and we won’t try to sell you anything.
