Rent Regulation & Adjustment Wizard

New
🌍 20 Countries 📅 2022–2026 data

Getting a rent increase wrong isn't just embarrassing — in many countries it can invalidate the increase entirely, or worse. Buurman now includes a built-in rent regulation database covering 20 countries, with official legal limits from 2022 through 2026, verified against government sources. The Netherlands, Germany, Portugal, France, Spain, Belgium, Ireland, Italy, Austria, the UK (including Scotland), the US (New York, Oregon, California), Canada (Ontario, BC, Québec), and more.

Open the new Rent Adjustments section, pick your year, and see the legal ceiling for your region. Berlin's Kappungsgrenze, Flemish health index caps, Scottish rent control zones — all in there. The wizard then previews exactly what that means for each of your contracts, and lets you apply the adjustments across all of them in one click. If your country isn't covered yet, you can request it directly from the app.

Portfolio Investment Dashboard

New

Your properties are investments — now you can see how they're performing together. The new portfolio dashboard aggregates your entire portfolio into one view: total value, equity, monthly cash flow, net operating income, cap rate, cash-on-cash return, debt coverage ratio, and occupancy — all in one place, automatically updated.

Seven interactive charts let you compare property performance side by side, see how your portfolio is allocated by category and country, track cash flow trends over time, and understand how your equity is composed. When your numbers are ready for the bank or accountant, export a full PDF or CSV summary with one click.

Dutch WWS Points Calculator

New
🇳🇱 Netherlands only

Know exactly where your rental property stands under Dutch law. The new WWS calculator computes the official Woningwaarderingsstelsel score for any of your properties — Buurman pre-fills what it already knows (surface area, energy label, rooms, parking), and you fill in the rest. You get the official point score with a full category breakdown, plus an instant classification: below 143 is regulated, 144–186 is mid-segment, 187+ is free sector. Year-versioned rules for 2023–2026. Save calculations and track how your score changes over time directly on the property page.
