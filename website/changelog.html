Release headline

Buildings With More Than One Front Door

Until now a property was one dwelling with one tenant. A property can now hold as many units as it really has, each with its own tenant, rent and contract — and the costs of the building split across them.

★ Spotlight · Headliner

Units, for Buildings That Are More Than One Home

New

If you own a house split into four flats, you have been fighting Buurman: four properties at one address, or one property that could only ever hold one tenant. Now a property is the building and a unit is each thing you let inside it.

A Units tab on any building — a grid of every unit with its number, tenant, rent, and whether it is let or standing empty, and a running “three of four let” at the top. Each unit opens its own page with its own characteristics, amenities and rooms.

Split an existing property in one go — say how many units, pick the numbering you actually use on the doors (1, 2, 3 · A, B, C · 1.01, 1.02) and a starting floor. Everything already recorded stays on the first unit.

Building costs split themselves — log a €1,200 roof repair against the building and Buurman divides it across the units, by floor area, evenly, or by percentages you set once. Any single expense can be overridden by hand. Change the rule and past splits stay put, because service‑charge statements are documents you have already sent.

Contracts, occupancy and vacancy are per unit — a lease is attached to the unit it covers, not the postcode, so occupancy is the share of units let rather than a building being counted as full because one flat has a tenant.

Plans count units. A single house or flat is one unit, exactly as before. A building with five flats is five. Nothing changes for you if you let whole properties.

Single‑dwelling landlords will notice almost nothing: every property already has one unit behind the scenes, and the Units tab only appears once there is more than one.

New

Buurman on your phone is now a phone app — not a desktop app squeezed onto a small screen. We rebuilt every list, dashboard, form, chart, and modal around your thumb. Fifty‑plus upgrades, end to end — the biggest single release in Buurman’s history.
