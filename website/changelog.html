What's New

Every week we ship improvements to make your life as a landlord easier. Here's what we've been building.

Week of February 17, 2026
v0.7 → v0.23

Property Investment Dashboard

New

Your properties are investments — now Buurman treats them that way. Every property gets its own analytics dashboard with ROI, Cap Rate, Cash-on-Cash Return, Net Operating Income, and more. Interactive charts reveal cash flow trends, occupancy patterns, and expense breakdowns. Add your purchase price and mortgage details, and Buurman projects your returns six months into the future. Export polished PDF or CSV reports for your accountant, bank, or partners.

Commercial, Industrial & Agricultural Properties

New

Buurman is no longer just for apartments and houses. Manage offices, warehouses, retail spaces, farmland, vineyards, and mixed-use buildings — each with category-specific details, property types, and amenities. Whether you own a studio flat or a logistics park, it all lives in one place.

Rent History & Period Tracking

New

Rent changes over time — now Buurman tracks every adjustment. See a visual timeline of rent periods on each contract, record increases or decreases with effective dates, and pending payments automatically update to match. Your property booklet PDF now includes the full rent history too.

Financial Reports, Reimagined

Improved

Reports now come with flexible time periods — month, quarter, year, all time, or any custom range you choose. Filter across multiple properties, switch between chart types, and export everything as CSV or PDF with one click.

Bulk Data Entry

New

Stop entering expenses one by one. Copy rows straight from your spreadsheet and paste them into Buurman — columns are detected automatically. Set categories per row, and smart date parsing handles DD/MM and MM/DD formats based on your preferences. "Continue adding" mode keeps your property and category pre-filled between saves. Bulk uploads now send just one summary notification instead of flooding your inbox.

Multi-Currency Support

New

Managing properties across borders? Every monetary field now carries its own currency. A property purchased in GBP with rent collected in EUR and expenses paid in PLN just works. Built-in currency pickers backed by the ISO 4217 standard, with automatic decimal precision when switching currencies.

Smarter Payment Workflows

Improved

A new "Register Payment" flow lets you record rent already received — one click and it's marked as paid. Schedule future payments, mark them paid on creation, filter by contract, and delete records you no longer need. Built around how landlords actually work.

Drag-and-Drop Map Pins

Improved

When automatic geocoding can't find your property's exact location, you can now place the pin manually by clicking or dragging on the map. Works for both property and tenant addresses — no more "Location Not Found" dead ends.

New Expense Categories & Notification Controls

New

Track Fees, Property Taxes, and Mortgage Payments as dedicated expense categories. Plus, you now control which notification channels are active on your account — toggle email and SMS independently, while security-critical messages always reach you.

Quality of Life

Improved
  • Cmd+Enter submits any form instantly
  • Keyboard & swipe navigation in document and photo previews — use arrow keys or swipe on mobile
  • Tabs remember your position when navigating back
  • Better currency formatting with proper symbols everywhere
  • Configurable operating cost due months — annual costs like insurance or HOA can be placed in the correct month on your dashboard instead of being spread evenly

Bug Fixes

Fixed
  • Settings page no longer loads blank on navigation
  • Multiple payments per day on the same contract now work correctly
  • Theme preference changes no longer cause a conflict error
  • Property edit forms properly load all financial fields
  • Chart axes now display the year for clarity
