The Contract Paper Trail Does Itself Now

New

Sending a rent increase notice used to mean opening a Word template, getting one date wrong, and finding out three months later the notice didn’t legally hold. Buurman now generates consistently formatted PDFs directly from your data — every name, date, amount, and clause filled in from your contract data.

Contract extension addendum — the moment a renewal is approved, a complete extension letter is ready: original terms, new end date, updated rent, a place to sign.

Rent change notice — a notice that meets the formal requirements tenants and courts expect, showing the effective date and both the old and new amounts — everything a tenant needs to understand the change.

Every document is attached to the contract’s record — ready to prove it was issued if it ever comes into question. Confirm that local regulations are met for your jurisdiction.

Buurman Now Speaks 13 Languages

New

No more sending formal notices in the wrong language and hoping for the best. Every screen, form, error message, and tooltip is now available in English, Dutch, Portuguese, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, Polish, and Greek.

Each team member picks their own language independently. The team admin sets a default that drives all outgoing emails and generated PDFs — so a tenant in Lisbon gets correspondence in Portuguese while your accountant in Amsterdam works in Dutch.

Nothing to configure per document. Language for outgoing documents follows the default your admin sets — across contract letters, rent change notices, and email notifications.

Bulk Import Contacts from Excel & CSV

New

Upload a spreadsheet and bring in hundreds of contacts — tenants, guarantors, anyone — in one go. A drag-and-drop mapping UI matches your columns to the right fields — review and adjust before anything is saved. A full preview shows exactly what will be imported.

If anything looks off, you get a line-by-line error report before a single record is saved. Duplicate emails within your team are flagged before import. Works with CSV and Excel files.

Improved
  • Contact balance at a glance — contact cards now show a colour-coded badge: green for all paid, amber for upcoming, red for overdue, blue for guarantors. See who needs attention without opening a thing
  • Branded password reset email — password reset emails now look like Buurman, not a generic system notification
  • Smarter back navigation — going back from a contact, property, or contract page now drops you exactly where you left off — scroll position, active tab, and all

Retroactive Rent Adjustments

New

Sometimes a rent increase needs to apply to a period that's already passed — a late government index publication, a delayed negotiation, or a correction. Previously, you had to manually calculate the difference. Now Buurman handles it automatically: apply a rent increase to past periods, and Buurman automatically creates adjustment payments for the difference on any already-paid rent. You'll see a clear preview of what will happen before confirming, including how many retroactive adjustments will be made.

If anything goes wrong while applying changes across multiple contracts, Buurman shows you exactly which ones succeeded and which still need attention — no guessing, no risk of double-charging.

Rent Regulation & Adjustment Wizard

New
🌍 20 countries 📅 2022–2026 data

Raising rent shouldn't require a law degree — but getting it wrong can mean fines, disputes, or a voided increase. Buurman now includes a built-in rent regulation database covering 20 countries, with legal limits from 2022 through 2026, cross-referenced against official government sources. The Netherlands, Germany, Portugal, France, Spain, Belgium, Ireland, Italy, Austria, the UK (including Scotland), the US (New York, Oregon, California), Canada (Ontario, BC, Québec), and more.

Open the new Rent Adjustments section, pick your year, and see the legal ceiling for your region. Berlin's Kappungsgrenze, Flemish health index caps, Scottish rent control zones — all accounted for. The wizard previews exactly what each adjustment means for your contracts, then lets you apply them all in one click. If your country isn't covered yet, you can request it directly from the app.

Covered countries: Netherlands, Germany, Portugal, France, Spain, Belgium, Ireland, Italy, Austria, United Kingdom (including Scotland), Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Poland, Switzerland, Czech Republic, Luxembourg — plus the United States (New York, Oregon, California) and Canada (Ontario, British Columbia, Québec)

Dutch WWS Points Calculator

New
🇳🇱 Netherlands only

Know exactly where your rental property stands under Dutch law. The new WWS calculator computes the official Woningwaarderingsstelsel score for any of your properties — Buurman pre-fills what it already knows (surface area, energy label, rooms, parking), and you fill in the rest. You get the point score with a full category breakdown and an instant classification: below 143 points is regulated rent, 144–186 is mid-segment, 187 and above is free sector. Year-versioned rules for 2023–2026. Save calculations and track how your score changes over time directly on the property page.
