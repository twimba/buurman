Properties & Portfolio

Every property, one dashboard. Details, photos, documents, and a full investment view — ROI, cap rate, cash flow charts, and side-by-side comparison across your entire portfolio.

Contacts & Relationships

Tenants, contractors, service providers — everyone connected to your properties, in one place. Add notes with follow-up reminders, tag contacts, and track who's linked to whom.

Contracts & Renewals

The full contract lifecycle — create, renew, extend. Set automatic or manual renewals, track rent adjustments, and use advisory defaults for 7 European countries to speed up setup.

Finances & Reports

Income, expenses, and payments — tracked and tidy. Export transaction data to Excel, CSV, or PDF, and get notified when payments are overdue so nothing slips through.

Rent Regulation

Rent increase guidance for 20 countries. Built-in rate limits, a step-by-step adjustment wizard, and retroactive corrections — stay informed without needing a law degree.

Secure & Private

Your data belongs to you. Full stop. Export everything with Buurman Takeout, erase contact data for GDPR compliance, or download your portfolio as PDF and Excel. No lock-in, ever.

Notifications & Reminders

Overdue payments, expiring contracts, upcoming renewals — Buurman alerts you by email or SMS before things slip through the cracks. Smart digests bundle multiple alerts into one message, so your inbox stays clean.

Documents & Photos

Contracts, invoices, inspection photos — securely stored and linked to the right property or contact. Access them from anywhere, and your whole team sees the same files automatically.

Built to Evolve

We ship improvements every week, shaped by what landlords actually ask for. Early access means your feedback directly influences what we build next.
