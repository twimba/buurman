What’s in it

Built for landlords who'd rather collect rent than wrestle software.

Properties

Photos, documents, and the numbers that tell you whether it was a good buy: ROI, cap rate, cash flow. A building can hold several units, each with its own tenant, rent and contract, and a shared cost like a roof repair splits across them. A chart ranks everything you own by what it returns, so the one dragging is obvious.

Contacts

Tenants, the plumber, the accountant, whoever services the boiler. Keep notes on them, set a reminder to follow something up, and see which property each one is attached to. Give a tenant their own language and Buurman emails them in it.

Contracts

Create a lease, renew it, extend it. Renewals can run on their own or wait for you to approve them. Rent adjustments are tracked, and there are sensible starting defaults for seven European countries.

Money

What came in, what went out, what’s still owed. Any of it exports to Excel, CSV, Google Sheets or PDF. You get an email when rent is late.

Rent rules

Rent increase guidance for 20 countries, with the legal caps already in. There’s a wizard for putting a rise through, and it’ll correct one retroactively if you got it wrong.

Your data

The data is yours. Take all of it out at once with Buurman Takeout, push the portfolio into Google Sheets, or download PDFs and spreadsheets. When a tenant asks you to erase their details, you can do that properly.

Reminders

Email or SMS when rent goes overdue or a contract is about to expire. If several things happen at once they turn up as one message instead of six.

Documents

Leases, invoices, the photos from the last inspection. Each file is attached to a property or a person, and everyone on your team sees the same thing.

What comes next

Buurman is early. Most of what gets built next comes out of emails from the landlords already using it, so if something you need is missing, tell us.
