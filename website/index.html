What is Buurman? 🤔

Your friendly neighborhood property management solution (see what we did there?)

Buurman (Dutch for "neighbor") is a modern property management platform designed specifically for small landlords. We built this because enterprise software for property management is like using a flamethrower to light a candle. 🔥

Whether you own one apartment or a small portfolio, Buurman helps you stay organized, track finances, manage tenants, and grow your rental business—all from a simple, intuitive dashboard. No PhD required. Promise! 🎓

Property Management

Tenant Tracking

Manage tenant information, lease agreements, and communication history. Never miss an important date.

Financial Reports

Track rental income, expenses, and generate professional financial reports for tax season in seconds.

Document Storage

Secure & Private
